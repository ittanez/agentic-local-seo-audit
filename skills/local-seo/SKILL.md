# Local SEO Audit Framework

## GBP Complete Audit
- Claimed/verified? Primary + secondary categories? No keyword stuffing?
- Address, phone, URL, hours (including holidays) accurate?
- Description (750 chars, keyword-rich)?
- All services and products listed?
- Photos: 50+ qty, high quality, variety (exterior, interior, team, work)?
- Videos, 360° tour?
- Posts: weekly minimum, variety (updates, offers, events)?
- Q&A seeded? Messaging enabled? Booking configured?
- All relevant attributes selected?

## Review Ecosystem
- Count, rating, velocity, recency vs. competitors
- Content quality, keyword presence, photo reviews
- Negative review themes and patterns
- Owner responses: every review? Personalized? Timely?
- Multi-platform: Yelp, Facebook, Trustpilot, industry sites

## NAP Consistency
- Exact consistency across: website, GBP, all citations, social, schema
- Old addresses/phones still live anywhere?

## Citations
- Core: Google, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, Facebook, BBB
- Data aggregators: Data Axle, Neustar, Foursquare
- Industry-specific, local directories
- Accuracy, completeness, duplicates, competitor comparison

## Local Landing Pages
- Unique content per location (NOT template swaps)
- Local context, map, testimonials, images, FAQs, NAP
- Service × Location combination pages

## Local Link Building
- Local media, bloggers, charities, events, sports teams
- Chamber of Commerce, associations, sponsorships

## Local SERP Rankings
- Local Pack, Google Maps, organic, Apple/Bing Maps
