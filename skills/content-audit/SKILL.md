# Content Audit Framework

## Content Inventory & Quality
- Catalog every page: URL, title, type, word count, keyword, quality score (1-10)
- Identify: thin content (<300 words), duplicates (internal + external),
  outdated content, cannibalization, AI-generated low-quality content

## Content Strategy Assessment
- Pillar + cluster model in place? Publishing frequency?
- Content type diversity (blogs, guides, FAQs, case studies, videos, tools)?
- Funnel coverage (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU)? Local content? Evergreen balance?

## Service Pages (Per Service)
- Dedicated page exists? 1500+ words? Covers: what, process, benefits,
  pricing, FAQs, testimonials, case studies? Location-modified versions?

## Location Pages (Per Location)
- Dedicated page? UNIQUE content (not template swaps)?
- Local context, embedded map, local testimonials, real local photos?
- Service × Location matrix pages?

## Blog/Resource Content
- Targeting researched keywords? Satisfying intent?
- Linking to service pages? Comprehensive enough to rank?
- Author bylines? Updated regularly?

## FAQ Content
- FAQ page + distributed FAQs on relevant pages?
- Real customer questions (GSC, GBP Q&A, PAA)?
- FAQ schema implemented?

## Content for AI Extraction
- Clear definitions and explanations? Structured Q&A?
- Citation-worthy statements with data? Logical sections?
