# Content Gap Analysis Framework

## Topic Gaps
- Topics ALL competitors cover but client doesn't (table-stakes)
- Topics only #1 competitor covers (advantage to replicate)
- Missing TOFU/MOFU/BOFU content
- Missing seasonal, trending, and local topics

## Content Format Gaps
- Missing: guides, how-tos, calculators, tools, comparison pages
- Missing: videos, infographics, podcasts, case studies
- Missing: "X vs Y" pages, "best X in [location]" pages, cost guides

## Golden Opportunities (Highest Value)
- Blue ocean topics (no competitor covers + real search demand)
- Rising trends no one has addressed
- PAA questions with no good answers
- Unowned featured snippets
- Adjacent topics with high traffic potential

## Improvement Opportunities
- Thin pages → comprehensive guides
- Position 5-20 pages → top 3 with improvements
- Declining pages needing refresh
- High impression / low CTR pages (title/meta fixes)
- Cannibalization → consolidation
