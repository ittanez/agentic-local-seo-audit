# AI Visibility & AI SEO Audit Framework

## AI Overviews Audit
- Test top 20-30 keywords: AIO present? Client cited? Competitors cited?
- What format/structure gets cited? Highest-opportunity targets?

## Google AI Mode Audit
- Test key queries: business mentioned? Info accurate? Competitors?

## LLM Visibility Audit
Test across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot:
- "[Service] in [Location]" queries
- "Best [provider type] in [location]"
- "[Business Name]" — info accuracy
- Document per query × platform: mentioned? accurate? competitors?

## AEO Assessment
- Clear definitions/explanations? FAQ format? Question-answer content?
- Featured snippet optimization? PAA coverage? Voice-compatible?

## GEO Assessment
- Citeability: specific, factual, quotable statements?
- Source authority: E-E-A-T strong enough for AI trust?
- Uniqueness: original research/data AI can't find elsewhere?
- Structure: clear headers, extractable sections, key takeaways?
- Entity optimization: entities defined with relationships?
- Schema: comprehensive structured data AI can parse?
