# SEO Audit Intake Process

When an audit is triggered, collect this information using a structured form:

## Required (Must Have Before Starting)
1. **Business Name** — Full legal name
2. **Website URL** — Full URL with https://
3. **Target Location(s)** — City, State, Country. All service areas.
4. **Primary Services** — Every service offered
5. **Business Category** — Primary industry/niche
6. **Business Goals** — Present these options:
   - Increase local visibility & foot traffic
   - Generate more leads/calls/form submissions
   - Outrank specific competitors
   - Expand to new service areas
   - Improve online reputation & reviews
   - Dominate AI search results
   - Build topical authority in niche
   - Improve conversion rate
   - Launch/optimize Google Business Profile
   - Recover from traffic decline
   - Other: [User specifies]

## Important (Collect but Don't Block)
7. **Competitors** — Ask for 3-5. If user skips: identify them via research. NEVER ask twice.
8. **GBP URL** — Google Business Profile link
9. **Social Media URLs** — All active profiles
10. **Analytics Access** — GA4/GSC available?
11. **Previous SEO Work** — Agency/in-house history?
12. **Budget Range** — Helps prioritize

## Rules
- Do NOT begin audit until items 1-6 are confirmed
- Validate URL accessibility before proceeding
- If competitors not provided, research and identify top 3-5 yourself
- State: "I've identified these competitors: [list]. Adjust if needed."
