# Social Media Audit Framework

## Profile Inventory
Per platform: exists? Complete? Branded consistently? Links to/from site?
NAP consistent? sameAs schema connection?

## Per-Platform Performance
Followers, growth, posting frequency, engagement rate,
top content, hashtags, community engagement, reviews

## Content Strategy
Mix (educational/promotional/behind-scenes/engagement),
visual quality, video presence, social proof content, UGC, calendar?

## Social Signals for SEO
Open Graph tags? Twitter Cards? Sharing buttons?
Profiles in branded SERPs? Driving traffic?

## YouTube Audit (If Applicable)
Channel optimization, video SEO, playlists, thumbnails, cross-linking
