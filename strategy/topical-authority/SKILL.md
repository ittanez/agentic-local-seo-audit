# Topical Authority Audit Framework

## Current Authority Scoring (Per Core Topic)
- Depth (1-10): Surface vs. expert level?
- Breadth (1-10): Narrow vs. comprehensive subtopic coverage?
- Freshness (1-10): Updated within 12 months?
- Quality (1-10): Generic vs. genuinely expert with unique insights?
- Recognition: Featured snippets? PAA? Knowledge panels?
- Entity association: Business linked to core topics by Google?
